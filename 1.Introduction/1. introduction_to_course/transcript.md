# Introduction to the course

## Summary

The speaker, Aaron Francis, introduces himself as the instructor for the "Introduction to Postgres" course, describing himself as a database educator, software developer, and father. He outlines his primary goals for the course: making the content fun, valuable, and profitable for students while respecting their time.

Key aspects of the course include:

- **Objective:** To transfer the instructor's enthusiasm for databases to the students or, at a minimum, make databases less intimidating.
- **Curriculum:** The course covers creating efficient tables, writing effective queries, utilizing indexes, and working with JSON.
- **Format:** The instruction will involve screen work and drawings.
- **Cost & Sponsorship:** The entire course is 100% free, sponsored by Supabase as a contribution to the community. The instructor emphasizes that the content focuses on core Postgres, not just Supabase-specific features.

Francis concludes by encouraging feedback and expressing his hope that students find significant value in the material.
