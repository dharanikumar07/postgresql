# Why choose Postgres

## Summary

This video outlines several compelling reasons to choose Postgres for database management, categorizing them into "social" factors and technical advantages.

### Social Reasons for Choosing Postgres

- **Popularity & Ecosystem:** Postgres is currently experiencing a surge in popularity. While chasing fads isn't always ideal, the influx of new users means a growing ecosystem.
- **Community Alignment:** Many programming communities (Ruby on Rails, JavaScript frameworks, and increasingly PHP) favor Postgres. Sticking with what your community uses ensures easier access to resources, consultants, and hiring pools.
- **Managed Hosting Availability:** Because of its popularity, there is a wide array of reliable third-party hosts available, making it easy to outsource database management.

### Technical Reasons for Choosing Postgres

- **Reliability:** Having been around since 1986, Postgres is extremely mature. It has resolved most edge cases and offers high confidence in data persistence and lack of corruption.
- **Extensibility:** Unlike MySQL, Postgres functions almost like a platform. It supports thousands of extensions for various needs, including full-text search, vector embeddings, time series (Timescale), and geographical data (PostGIS).
- **Data Types:** Postgres offers superior support for diverse data types compared to MySQL and SQLite. It includes roughly 40 data types (such as native UUID, ranges, JSON, and geo support), allowing developers to more accurately model reality.
- **Vibrant Community:** The Postgres community is truly open-source and welcoming, unlike MySQL (owned by Oracle). This results in extensive documentation, educational resources, and active support channels.
